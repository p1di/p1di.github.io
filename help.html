Help & Support

How do I switch servers if a player isn't loading?
Open the app popup, click the Server dropdown, and choose another server. You can also click ⚡ Check Links to automatically test server availability.
Why am I seeing popups on external servers?
External video hosts may serve ads. We strongly recommend installing an adblocker. See our Adblocker Guide for instructions.
Can I add my own custom streaming sources?
Yes! Inside the player controls, click + Add Source to save your own embed URL pattern.
