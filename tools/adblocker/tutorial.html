How to Install an Adblocker

Third-party video streaming players often contain aggressive popups and redirects. Installing a trusted adblocker prevents popups and keeps your browser safe.

Recommended Adblockers

uBlock Origin
The most efficient, lightweight open-source ad and pop-up blocker.

Brave Browser
Built-in automatic ad and tracker blocking with zero configuration needed.
Get Brave Browser →

Step-by-Step Installation

  1. Click on Get uBlock Origin (or search for uBlock Origin in your browser extension store).
  2. Click Add to Chrome (or Add to Firefox / Edge / Safari).
  3. Confirm the prompt by clicking Add Extension.
  4. Once installed, refresh your streaming page. All popups and ads will be blocked automatically!
